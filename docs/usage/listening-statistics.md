# Listening Statistics

Click **Statistics** in the sidebar to see what you've been listening to.

At the top, a row of library facts shows how many songs, albums, and artists you can play. If podcasts are turned on, it also shows the podcasts you subscribe to and their episodes. If radio is turned on, it shows the radio stations you can listen to. These numbers count everything you can play, not just this period, and follow your choice to include public media or not.

If you're the owner of the Koel installation, you'll also see **Storage used**: the total size of all song files in the library. Podcast episodes and transcoded files don't count. Songs added by older Koel versions may not have a size on record and count as zero.

Below the library facts, pick a period: the last 7 days, 30 days, 12 months, or all time. For that period, you'll see:

- how many plays, how much listening time, and how many different songs and artists;
- your plays over time;
- which days of the week and which hours of the day you listen most, in your own time zone;
- your top 12 songs and genres, and your top 10 artists and albums.

A song counts as played once you've listened to a quarter of it, the same way Koel counts plays everywhere else. Plays from [Subsonic clients](./subsonic) count too. Podcast episodes don't.

Koel starts counting plays for your statistics once you're on a version that has this page, so older listening doesn't show up here. Listening time adds up the full length of each song you played.
